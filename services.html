OWNER QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, straightforward answers about partnering with HYVE for property leasing, sales, short-term management, and asset care.

What makes HYVE different from traditional real estate brokers in Sri Lanka?

Traditional brokers focus merely on matching two parties for a one-off commission and then disappear. HYVE operates as an ongoing asset management partner. We take full fiduciary responsibility: managing day-to-day tenant relations, maintenance upkeep, utility payments, legal compliance, and hospitality turnover under founder-led supervision.

Can HYVE manage my property if I reside permanently overseas?

Yes, over 65% of our portfolio belongs to Sri Lankan expatriates living in the UK, Australia, the US, Canada, the UAE, and Singapore. We represent you locally with official authority, handle municipal council and tax payments, conduct bi-weekly photographic audits, and remit your net earnings directly to your chosen foreign or local bank accounts.

How does HYVE screen prospective long-term tenants and short-term guests?

For long-term leases, we carry out a strict 5-stage background vetting process verifying employment, corporate credentials, diplomatic status, previous landlord references, and financial standing. For short-term Airbnb stays, all guests must possess verified government identification, positive historical reviews, and adhere to strict house rules and damage deposit protocols.

How are maintenance emergencies and repair costs managed?

We maintain an on-call network of licensed electricians, plumbers, HVAC specialists, and painters. Minor maintenance under an agreed pre-authorized threshold is rectified promptly to safeguard the property. Any major expenditure requires your prior approval with itemized contractor quotes, and all completed work is backed by photographic verification and genuine tax invoices.

What are HYVE's management fees and payout timelines?

Our fee structures are transparent and performance-driven. For short-term stays, we operate on a competitive percentage of net booking revenue. For long-term leasing and full property management, we provide straightforward tiered plans. Payouts and digital financial statements are issued by the 5th working day of each calendar month.
